FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions from Village Mill families.

Visit the Booking page, select an available date on the calendar, then fill in your details and submit the form. You'll receive a confirmation email within 24 hours. You can also create an account to track your bookings in the dashboard.

Log in to your account and visit My Dashboard. You'll see all upcoming bookings with a Cancel button. Please cancel at least 24 hours in advance where possible. Alternatively, email us at hello@villagemillbabysitting.com.

Payment is arranged directly with your babysitter. Our standard rates start at $18/hour. The exact rate depends on the sitter's experience, the number of children, and the length of the session. We do not charge a platform fee.

Every sitter goes through an in-person interview, two reference checks, a full background check, and must hold valid CPR and First Aid certifications. We re-verify certifications annually.

Yes. When filling in a booking request, select your preferred sitter from the dropdown. Subject to their availability, we'll always try to match you with your preferred sitter. Returning families often build a great ongoing relationship with one sitter.

Yes. Select "Overnight" from the preferred time dropdown on the booking form. Overnight rates are agreed directly with the sitter. Please include any relevant details (sleep routines, emergency contacts) in the Special Notes field.

Several of our sitters have experience working with children with special needs. Please describe your child's requirements in the Special Notes field when booking, or email us and we'll match you with the most suitable sitter on our team.

Visit the Sign In page and click "Create Account". You can register with your email and a password, or sign in instantly with Google. Once logged in, you can view your booking history and manage upcoming appointments from your dashboard.
